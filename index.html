    Survival

    • Earn points for every hit and kill. Headshots and knife kills pay extra.
    • Spend points on wall weapons, doors to new zones, and perk machines.
    • Find the generator to restore power. Perks and the Mutation Forge need it.
    • The Xeno Cache (green light beam) gives a random weapon for 950. It moves when the hive claims it.
    • The Mutation Forge upgrades your weapon for 5000.
    • Every 5th round a swarm attacks. Every 8th, the Hive Tyrant comes.
    • Glowing drops: Max Ammo, Orbital Strike, Double Bounty, Insta-Kill, Fire Sale.
    • Mutated weapons carry a random mutation effect: Chain Shock, Blast Furnace, Cryo Nova, Gravity Well or Turned.
    • Each arena has traps, a Mutagen Capsule machine, 3 shield parts for the workbench, and boarded vents.
    • Some say the hive can be severed. Look for strange obelisks. Some say its eggs sing.
