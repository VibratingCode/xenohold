# Xenohold map design briefs

Every arena answers the same questions before anything is placed in it:

- **What is this place?** Who built it, for what, and who lived or worked here.
- **What happened here?** Before, the fall, and what is left now.
- **Why is each room here?** What it was for, what is in it because of that, and what it tells you now.
- **Do we want it functional?** Things that belong to the place (the armory's ammo, a pump's impeller, a machine shop's bench) do something. Things that do not belong get moved, reskinned or cut: an ancient temple has flame jets, not an electrified floor.
- **Why is the goal what it is?** Every map runs the same loop: *awaken* a set of objects, kill creatures near them until they are *saturated*, then the map's own ending. Each map gives that loop a reason that fits the place.

**Shared fiction for the objective loop.** The hive is one mind, spread across every body and growth. When a creature dies, a burst of its signal leaks out. The colony's machines, and the older things in the older places, can be tuned to catch that burst. Kill creatures next to them, and each death feeds them a piece of the swarm's signal until they can do their job against it. That is why you stand your ground by each one.

Anything here can be changed by hand in the map editor (`docs/EDITOR.md`); a map's saved edits take over from what this brief generates.

Each map below lists its zones with what belongs there, the story beats (wall scrawls, remains, logs you can read) and where the functional pieces go.

## Outpost Kestrel: LV-9 mining colony
**The place.** A helium-3 mine on a dead moon, about two hundred miners. It has a refinery, a barracks block, a command center with the comms gear, and a landing pad for the supply shuttle.
**What happened.** Three nights ago the refinery's shaft 3 broke into a warm cavity at 900 m. The swarm came up the shaft. The miners barricaded themselves in the barracks. The administrator called for evacuation, and the shuttle never came.
**The goal.** The hive grew over the *Comms Uplinks* and uses them to jam every frequency. Each kill near an uplink gives it a sample of the jamming signal to filter out. Once all the uplinks are clear the evac shuttle can home in, and you *hold the landing pad* while it lands.
- **Landing Pad** (start): the shuttle pad, painted with its landing markings, edge lights, a fuel bowser and cargo pallets. Duffel bags and cases are piled at the pad edge by people who waited for the shuttle. *Functional:* the Landing Tower platform overlooks the pad; the Electro-Grid is the pad's ground-power plates.
- **Loading Dock:** ore carts, pallets and a forklift where the refinery's output was staged.
- **Refinery:** ore tanks, a conveyor and ore carts. Shaft 3's hatch is torn open and hive creep spills out of it: this is where it started. *Functional:* the Wonder Bench is the refinery machine shop; the Shredder Fan is the refinery exhaust fan; the companion egg sits by the shaft.
- **Barracks:** bunks, lockers and a mess table, with a barricade of tables and mattresses. Someone lies under a blanket. Scrawl: *DON'T OPEN THE DOOR*. *Functional:* the ammo station is the colony security locker.
- **Command Center:** consoles showing *NO SIGNAL*, a map table and a radio. The administrator is slumped at the console. *Functional:* the Comms Hut is here, because it is comms.
- **Logs:** the refinery foreman ("We hit a cavity at 900 m. It was warm."), a miner in the barracks, and the administrator's last call.

## Char Depths: magma caverns, sublevel 4
**The place.** Caverns under a geothermal survey site. Hesperus Geothermal sent a team down to tap the magma. The Brood got there first: this is its oldest nest.
**What happened.** The survey team's lift cage fell. They made camp, and the caverns took them one by one. Some of them are still in the Spawning Pools.
**The goal.** *Psionic Nodes* are the hive's nervous system, and they feed on death. Force them to drink too much too fast and they overload, exposing the Hive Heart. Sever it and the Overmind comes up to meet you.
- **Survey Camp** (start): the collapsed lift cage, a burned tent, the survey drill, rope coils and flares. *Functional:* the ammo station is the team's supply cache, a dropped crate rather than a machine.
- **Magma Rift:** lava channels, basalt, and a mining laser abandoned mid-cut. *Functional:* the Magma Vent trap (the Electro-Grid reskinned).
- **Spawning Pools:** egg clusters and cocoons holding the survey team. *Functional:* the companion egg.
- **Tyrant's Nest:** a mound of bones, claw gouges and a pile of trophies (helmets).
- **Crystal Grotto:** giant crystal clusters and a survey marker. *Functional:* the Wonder Bench is the team's field bench.
- **No tech that could not be down here:** no turret nests, and no Shredder Fan.
- **Logs:** the geologist ("the rock is warm and it has a heartbeat"), the team lead's last radio call, and a note about the crystals.

## Frostfall Relay: deep-space relay
**The place.** A relay station that bounces the colony's signals into deep space. A crew of twelve works six-month rotations.
**What happened.** A signal came in from deep space and the relay amplified it. The Cryo Lab thawed a sample cut from the lake ice. It woke. The last transmission was a scream and forty seconds of chittering.
**The goal.** The *Relay Dishes* are drowned in chitter. Each kill near one teaches it the pattern to cancel. When the dishes are clean you can see where the hive broadcasts from: *Frozen Hive Nodes* grown into the ice. Destroy them and the relay sends the call for rescue.
- **Relay Yard** (start): the relay building, snow-buried vehicles and a flagpole. *Functional:* the Relay Shack is here.
- **Comms Array:** dishes, a cable run and a transmitter cabinet. *Functional:* the Antenna Deck platform; the Electro-Grid is the array's ground plane.
- **Frozen Lake:** the ice-core drill site with a sample crate, and a hole cut in the ice.
- **Hab Domes:** crew bunks, a galley and lockers. *Functional:* the ammo station.
- **Cryo Lab:** cryo cabinets, the thawing tank (empty, glass broken outward) and lab benches. *Functional:* the Wonder Bench; the companion egg, since the sample was a clutch.
- **Logs:** the station chief, the cryo tech ("it moved at -40"), and the relay operator's final message.

## Derelict Ark "Providence": colony ship
**The place.** A generation ark carrying 4,000 colonists in stasis to Hesperus, with a waking crew of thirty.
**What happened.** Cargo loaded at a waystation carried eggs. The hive learned to plug into the stasis pods and use the sleepers as incubators. The crew fought back. The ark drifts with its reactor at 14%.
**The goal.** The hive has wired itself into the *Stasis Pods*. Each creature that dies next to a pod breaks another strand of its hold. Free them all and the reactor can be reached. Then *rig the reactor valves* to scuttle the ship, and make the lifeboat.
- **Central Concourse** (start): benches and a directory sign, the Providence crest, a crew barricade and an evacuation route painted on the floor.
- **Crew Quarters:** bunk pods and personal effects. *Functional:* the ammo station is the crew armory locker.
- **Bridge:** the captain's chair, helm consoles, a star map and a broken viewport frame.
- **Reactor Core:** the core, coolant lines and radiation signs, with hive tissue feeding on the heat. *Functional:* the Shredder Fan is the coolant fan; the Wonder Bench is the reactor workshop.
- **Engine Room:** the lifeboat cradle and engine machinery. *Functional:* the companion egg is in a cracked cargo crate.
- **Logs:** the captain, the quartermaster (cargo manifest from the waystation), and a sleeper who woke early.

## Hesperus City: colony capital
**Already dressed district by district** (docks, neon, capitol, market, downtown, park, residential, metro, the crater). What it gains here: readable logs (a news anchor, a quarantine officer, a kid's note) and functional placement.
- **Functional:** the ammo station is in the Corner Store's gun counter (its building); the Wonder Bench is at the Metro maintenance depot; the companion egg is at the edge of the Hive Crater; the Electro-Grid is at the Capitol checkpoint.

## Dust Basin: open-pit quarry
**The place.** Basin Mining Co.'s open pit in a red canyon, running three shifts a day for iron and rare earths.
**What happened.** Pit 4 broke into a cavern. The vibration of the drills drew the Dune Borers, and something much bigger. The drills never stopped: the Titan's movement keeps them turning.
**The goal.** The *Survey Beacons* are seismic sensors. Every creature killed near one gives it a footstep to triangulate. When all of them agree, the Basin Titan's burrow is found and it is flushed out. Kill it and the drills can stop.
- **Loading Yard** (start): a haul truck, ore piles, fuel tanks and pallets. *Functional:* the Foreman Office; the ammo station is inside it (the blasting and ammo locker).
- **Dig Site:** the drill rig, survey stakes, blasting-charge crates, and the breach hole glowing at the pit floor. *Functional:* the Drill Platform.
- **Ore Crusher:** the crusher, its hopper and conveyor, ore heaps and spare gears. *Functional:* the Shredder Fan is the Crusher Rotor; the Wonder Bench is the crusher repair bench.
- **Canyon Floor:** boulders, a derailed ore cart and a bleached ribcage (the Titan sheds). *Functional:* the companion egg, in a burrow.
- **Bunkhouse:** bunks, a card table and lockers, with scrawl: *IT'S UNDER US*.
- **Relay Tower:** the radio mast, a generator and the radio desk. *Functional:* the Electro-Grid is the tower's ground grid.
- **Logs:** the shift foreman, the surveyor, and the radio operator.

## Verdant Rot: botanical research station
**The place.** Verdant Labs studied native plants to seed the colony's farms. Canopy walkways, a greenhouse, a specimen hall and a boat dock on the river.
**What happened.** The hive seeded the soil. Growth rose 400% in six days. The staff were taken into the jungle, and some are still in the trees, cocooned.
**The goal.** The *Seed Vaults* hold the clean seed bank. Awakened, their sterilizers learn the hive's spores from every creature that dies nearby. Once they are ready, carry *Spore Cores* (the hive's growth nuclei) into the vault's incinerator. The station burns its own jungle.
- **Research Atrium** (start): the reception desk, a botany exhibit, overgrown planters and a toppled sign reading *VERDANT LABS: GROWING TOMORROW*. *Functional:* the ammo station is the security desk.
- **Canopy Walk:** walkway posts, hanging vines and cocooned staff in the trees. *Functional:* the Canopy Deck platform.
- **Greenhouse:** planter rows, hydroponic trays and shattered panes. *Functional:* the Greenhouse building; the Shredder Fan is the ventilation fan.
- **Rot Pits:** rotting logs, fungus and bones. *Functional:* the companion egg.
- **Specimen Hall:** jars on shelves, a dissection table and broken cages. *Functional:* the Wonder Bench.
- **Overgrown Dock:** a half-sunk barge, dock posts and crates. *Functional:* the Electro-Grid is the dock's electric fence line.
- **Logs:** the head botanist, a graduate student, and the dock pilot.

## Helix Labs: genetics facility
**The place.** Helix Biotech's sublevel 2, where hive genes were spliced into soldiers (Project Chimera).
**What happened.** A specimen woke up. Containment failed from the inside. Someone restarted the reactor to keep the vats running.
**The goal.** The *Gene Sequencers* compile a kill code from every sample (kill) taken near them. Finished, the code shows which *Specimen Vats* are growing the next generation. Destroy them.
- **Lobby** (start): the reception desk, a security gate, waiting chairs and the Helix logo wall. *Functional:* the ammo station is the security checkpoint.
- **Cryo Storage:** cryo cabinets and nitrogen canisters. *Functional:* the Shredder Fan is the cooling fan.
- **Gene Lab:** lab benches, sequencers and a DNA display. *Functional:* the Wonder Bench.
- **Containment:** holding cells with the glass broken outward, restraints and warning lights. *Functional:* the companion egg (a specimen that imprinted on you).
- **Surgery:** the operating table and lamp, instrument trays, gurneys and blood.
- **Reactor Room:** the reactor column, coolant pipes and an alarm panel. *Functional:* the Electro-Grid is the coolant grid.
- **Logs:** the project lead, a surgeon, and the tech who restarted the reactor.

## The Undercroft: storm drains under Hesperus
**The place.** The capital's storm drains: brick tunnels, pump stations and the outfall to the river.
**What happened.** This is where the first eggs were laid. Maintenance crews reported them months before the city fell and were told to ignore them. Now the water is rising.
**The goal.** The *Pump Valves* are choked with egg-mass. Every creature that dies against an intake grate is chewed through, and the valve clears. Then *hold the pump station* while it drains the tunnels and opens the way up.
- **Junction** (start): signage, a manhole ladder with city light falling through, and pipes.
- **Overflow Tunnel:** flood debris and water-level marks on the walls.
- **Pump Station:** pump machines, valves and gauges. *Functional:* the Shredder Fan is the Pump Impeller.
- **Cistern:** egg clusters and cocoons: the first nest. *Functional:* the companion egg.
- **Maintenance:** a workbench, lockers, a maintenance cart and a *DAYS WITHOUT INCIDENT* board. *Functional:* the ammo station is the workers' lockers; the Wonder Bench.
- **Outfall:** the river grate and fog. *Functional:* the Electro-Grid is the flood gate's contact plates.
- **Logs:** the maintenance supervisor, a sewer worker ("the eggs are warm"), and a city memo telling them to ignore it.

## Glacier Deep: buried expedition
**The place.** The Colonial Science Directorate drilled 400 m into a glacier to reach something frozen at its heart.
**What happened.** They reached it. The Frost Wraith woke in the Mammoth Hall, where the enormous thing is still in the ice.
**The goal.** The expedition planned to collapse the tunnel behind them. The *Seismic Charges* are tuned to shatter hive ice, and each creature killed near one tunes it. Set them all off and the Frost Wraith comes out. Kill it, and what was frozen stays frozen.
- **Ice Chamber** (start): ice pillars, dead expedition lamps and a sled.
- **Frozen Falls:** a frozen waterfall and icicles.
- **Crystal Vault:** crystal clusters. *Functional:* the companion egg, frozen.
- **Buried Camp:** tents, a generator, core-sample racks and frozen sleeping bags. *Functional:* the ammo station is a supply crate; the Wonder Bench; the Electro-Grid is the heater grid.
- **Deep Crevasse:** rope and a fallen climber.
- **Mammoth Hall:** the enormous thing in the ice wall.
- **No Shredder Fan or turret nests** in an ice cave.
- **Logs:** the expedition leader, the glaciologist, and the driller.

## Crucible Foundry: volcanic smelter
**The place.** A foundry built on a volcanic vent to cast hull plating for colony ships.
**What happened.** The hive found the heat. The crucibles pour for it now: Slag Golems are cast, not born.
**The goal.** The hive sealed the *Furnace Valves* with resin. Bodies burning near a valve melt the seal. Open them all, *rig the furnaces* for a meltdown, and ride the freight lift out.
- **Casting Floor** (start): a ladle on chains, mold rows with glowing plates and rails. *Functional:* the Catwalk.
- **Slag Pits:** slag heaps with cooling crust. *Functional:* the companion egg, incubating in the heat.
- **Smelter:** furnaces, ore hoppers and ore piles. *Functional:* the Control Booth overlooks it; the Shredder Fan is the exhaust fan.
- **Cooling Yard:** stacked hull plates, a gantry and pallets. *Functional:* the ammo station.
- **Forge Hall:** anvils, the power hammer and tool racks. *Functional:* the Wonder Bench.
- **Crucible Core:** the great crucible. *Functional:* the Electro-Grid.
- **Logs:** the shift boss, a caster ("the metal is moving by itself"), and the plant manager.

- **Ossuary:** bone piles and skull walls.

**What happened.** The marines held it for eleven days. On day twelve no relief is coming. Every wall has claw marks at head height.
**The goal.** The *Signal Flare* posts were overrun. Each has to be held long enough to prime, and every kill near it buys the seconds. Then *hold the flare point* until relief arrives.
- **Courtyard** (start): a flagpole with a torn flag, a wrecked APC and piles of spent casings. *Functional:* the Electro-Grid is the perimeter shock mesh.
- **Motor Pool:** trucks, jerry cans and tire stacks. *Functional:* the Wonder Bench is the mechanic's bench; the Shredder Fan is the radiator fan.
- **Watchtower:** floodlights. *Functional:* the Guard Tower.

## Solid blocks and theme dressing

The layouts have solid blocks in the middle of some rooms (the `+` cells). They used to be shipping containers everywhere, which answered none of the questions above. Now each block is dressed for the room it stands in (`blocks` in `js/mapstory.js`). The dressing fills the block's whole footprint, so nothing you can see stops short of where you collide. Blocks in rooms not listed here keep the theme's look (containers on a landing pad or docking ring, hab domes in Frostfall's hab area, reactor cores on the ships, ruins in the temple, modules on Selene's pad).

| Map | Room | Block | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kestrel | Barracks | bunk modules (BERTHS) | two hundred miners slept somewhere |
| Kestrel | Command Center | uplink server racks | the comms gear the swarm is jamming |
| Bastion 9 | Barracks | bunk modules (SQUAD BAY) | a fire base sleeps its squads in the open bay |
| Bastion 9 | Motor Pool | an APC up on a service lift | it is the motor pool |
| Crucible | Smelter | furnace | it is the smelter |
| Crucible | Crucible Core / Forge Hall | induction unit / press | the machines each room is named for |
| Dust Basin | Ore Crusher / Relay Tower / Bunkhouse | crusher drive / relay racks / bunks | same |
| Frostfall | Comms Array / Relay Yard | signal processor / transformer | what a relay station runs on |
| Helix | Cryo Storage | back-to-back cryo pods | specimen storage |
| Helix | Gene Lab / Containment | specimen tanks (half smashed in Containment) | where things were grown, and got out |
| Ark | Crew Quarters | locker banks | crew storage |
| Ark | Central Concourse | garden beds under grow lights | a colony ship carries its seed stock |
| Verdant | Greenhouse | garden beds, some dead | it is a greenhouse |
| Selene | Solar Farm / Mass Driver | inverters / capacitor banks | what the panels and the driver feed |
| Starfall | Crash Site / Engine Wreck | torn hull, still burning by the engine | pieces of your ship |
| Undercroft | Pump Station / Cistern | pump housing / cistern pillar | what holds up and drains the city |

**Theme dressing by room.** Each theme also scatters its own props (derricks, specimen tanks, solar panels, furnaces, crates). Some of those only make sense in certain rooms, so `generic` in `js/mapstory.js` limits them. For example, derricks stand at the dig site and in the canyon, not in the bunkhouse. Specimen tanks stay in the labs, solar panels on the solar farm, and wall furnaces in the hot rooms. Supply crates stay in the armory, motor pool, hangar and camp stores, and the crashed ship's hull plates stay where it came down.
